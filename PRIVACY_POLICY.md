# Privacy Policy for Ink Ridge

**Last updated: August 23, 2026**

## Overview

Ink Ridge is a VR meditation and exploration game for Meta Quest 3. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

## Data Collection

**Ink Ridge does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.**

- No personal information is collected
- No analytics or tracking SDKs are included
- No advertising SDKs are included
- No account or login system exists
- No internet connection is required

## Local Data

All game progress (meditation records, walking time, comfort settings) is stored locally on your device using Unity's PlayerPrefs and local file storage. This data:

- Never leaves your device
- Is not shared with any third party
- Can be cleared by uninstalling the app

## Permissions

Ink Ridge requires the following permissions:

- **VR rendering**: Required for VR display on Meta Quest 3
- **Audio playback**: For ambient sounds and breathing guidance audio
- **Local storage**: For saving meditation progress

No camera, microphone, location, or network permissions are used.

## Children's Privacy

Ink Ridge is rated E (Everyone) and does not collect any data from users of any age.
